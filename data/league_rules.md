# Metro City Premier League — Official Rulebook (2026 Season)

## 1. Overview
The Metro City Premier League (MCPL) is a fictional 12-team indoor football
league. All rules below are fictional and used for demonstration only.

## 2. Match Format
- Each match consists of two halves of 25 minutes each, with a 10-minute
  half-time break.
- A team fields 6 players on the pitch at any time: 1 goalkeeper and 5
  outfield players.
- If a match ends level in the knockout stage, two 5-minute periods of extra
  time are played, followed by a penalty shootout if still level.

## 3. Scoring
- A goal scored from open play is worth 1 point.
- A goal scored directly from a corner kick (an "olimpico") is worth 2 points.
- Own goals count as 1 point to the opposing team.

## 4. Fouls and Penalties
- A yellow card is shown for reckless challenges. Two yellow cards in one match
  result in a red card.
- A red card results in the player being sent off, and the team plays with one
  fewer player for the remainder of the match.
- A player who receives a red card is automatically suspended for the next 1
  match. A violent-conduct red card carries a 3-match suspension.

## 5. Substitutions
- Each team may make up to 5 substitutions per match.
- Substitutions are "rolling": a substituted player may return to the pitch
  later in the same match.
- The goalkeeper may be substituted at any stoppage in play.

## 6. Player Eligibility
- A player must be at least 16 years old on the first day of the season to be
  eligible.
- Each squad may register a maximum of 18 players and a minimum of 10 players.
- A player may only be registered to one club per season.

## 7. Standings and Tie-Breakers
- Teams earn 3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss.
- If two teams are level on league points, the tie is broken in this order:
  1. Goal difference
  2. Total goals scored
  3. Head-to-head result
  4. A one-off playoff match at a neutral venue

## 8. Discipline and Appeals
- A club may appeal a red card within 48 hours of the final whistle.
- The Disciplinary Committee must rule on an appeal within 5 business days.
- Frivolous appeals may be fined up to 500 league credits.
